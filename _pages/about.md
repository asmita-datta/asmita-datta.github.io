I am a Marie Skłodowska-Curie Doctoral Fellow in Physics at the [University of Padova](https://www.unipd.it/en) and the [University of Cyprus](https://www.ucy.ac.cy/?lang=en), part of the [AQTIVATE](https://aqtivate.ucy.ac.cy/) doctoral network and based in the [Quantum Information and Matter Group](https://quantum.dfa.unipd.it).

I benchmark quantum algorithms, such as the Quantum Fourier Transform and Shor's algorithm, on tensor-network quantum circuit emulators, studying how errors propagate and how far classical simulation can scale.

{% details Current research %}
I study the performance and scalability of algorithms such as the Quantum Fourier Transform and Shor's algorithm using MPS and TTN simulations, with attention to error propagation, simulation quality, and computational efficiency. See my [projects](/projects/) for more.
{% enddetails %}

{% details Software %}
I contribute to the [Quantum TEA](https://www.quantumtea.it) software ecosystem, including [qtealeaves](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.10498928) and [qmatchatea](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.11619265).
{% enddetails %}

{% details Background %}
Before my PhD, I completed a BS-MS Dual Degree in Physics, with a minor in Data Science and Engineering, at the [Indian Institute of Science Education and Research, Bhopal](http://iiserb.ac.in/). My [MS thesis](https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.14153.51042) explored quantum machine learning methods for determining exchange-correlation potentials in many-body systems, combining quantum and artificial neural networks with first-principles data.

I have also worked on quantum networks, quantum many-body systems, and machine learning for the physical sciences through research stays at IISER Bhopal, the University of Freiburg, and the London Mathematical Laboratory Summer School.
{% enddetails %}
